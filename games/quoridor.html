Quoridor

A two-player race across the board. On your turn, move your pawn one square or place a wall to slow your opponent — first pawn to the far side wins.

Player 1 to move
Player 1 — 10 walls
Player 2 — 10 walls

Walls: switch to Place Wall, pick an orientation (or press R to flip), then click a groove intersection. A wall can never seal off a player's only path to the far side.
